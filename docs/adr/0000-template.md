# ADR-0000: Title as a sentence in the present tense

**Status:** Proposed
**Date:** YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

What forces are in play? What is true about the world that makes this decision necessary?
State the constraint, not the solution. If there is prior art — in this codebase or in another
product — name it and say what it does.

Include the facts a reader would otherwise have to go and find: version numbers, defaults,
protocol limits, measured numbers.

## Decision

What we are doing, in the active voice. One paragraph if possible.

## Alternatives considered

For each: what it was, and the specific reason it lost. An ADR with no rejected alternatives is
usually a decision that did not need recording.

## Consequences

What becomes easy. What becomes hard. What we are now committed to. What we will have to
revisit, and what would trigger revisiting it.

Be honest about the costs — an ADR that only lists benefits is marketing.

## References

Links to source, specs, benchmarks, or the discussion this came from.
